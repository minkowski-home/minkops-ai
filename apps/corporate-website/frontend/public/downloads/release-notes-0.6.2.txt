Minkops 0.6.2 — Windows release candidate — 11 October 2026

Windows 10/11 x64. Unsigned installer; Windows may show a publisher warning.
Default service: https://app.minkops.com

Source Discovery collects complete masters for all loaded Tally companies and
detailed vouchers for the selected inclusive period through local HTTP/XML.
Voucher Register exports honor date boundaries instead of the current Day Book date.
Confirmed, versioned context is archived locally and supplies subsequent work.
Excel discovery projects workbook structure and headers without business rows.

Bill Entry identifies the buyer's company from source evidence, preserves explicit
company selection, and verifies approved writes independently. Ambiguous decisions
and unsupported structures remain in shared attention. Best-guess writes retain
their review obligation; manual accounting corrections stay in Tally or Excel.

Recovery waits for all observed numeric company identities after one controlled
restart. Uncertain imports are reconciled without automatic replay. Native
licence/login screens require an operator handoff. No automatic update is installed.

Sign in, connect this PC, and open the intended companies in Tally with its local
HTTP/XML service on port 9000. Workspace workflows must be configured. Start with
an isolated test company and keep recovery backups until saves are verified.
Windows 10 compatibility is retained; its device test is deferred for this release.
