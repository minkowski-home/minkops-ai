The generic bounded surface: white, 4px radius, 1px border, no shadow.

```jsx
<Card>…</Card>
<Card tone="sunken" pad="var(--sp-5)">…</Card>
<Card accentEdge>Needs your approval</Card>
```

Do not stack shadows or nest more than two cards. `accentEdge` carries meaning (human action required) — do not use it decoratively.
